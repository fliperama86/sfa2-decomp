## Port: the drive goes on over the sectors between a music track's audio (2026-10-12)

- What was reported: issue 279 (music stops playing after a certain time; seen in training mode on one stage) and issue 258 (the game does not go on after the screen of the score ranking; the last lines of its log are two `audio: a stream starts` lines, at the sectors 138671 and 138667).
- Cause, one for both, in `port/src/cd.c`. The game ends a track by the drive's position alone: `check_cd_position_and_set_command_state` (`ps1/src/m14ff20_r2.c`) asks `GetlocP` at every pass and, once the position is above the track's end, stops the track or starts it again. The disc's audio files hold eight channels in turn, and where a channel's track is shorter than its file its places hold sectors with the submode 0, which are no audio. The disc layer handed such a sector over as a data sector and then, because the game has no ready handler during music, applied its rule for a reader that polls: the next sector waits until `CdReady` has taken the last one. The game's music never calls `CdReady` (`ps1/src/s14f9f0_r6.c` calls `CdSync` only), so the drive stood at the first such sector for ever and the position never passed the track's end.
- The tracks, read from the user's disc in place (private, nothing copied; the tables are the program's words at `0x8017d9e4` and `0x8017eb44`, read as `start_cd_track_from_code` in `ps1/src/m14ff20_r3.c` uses them): eight audio files, at the sectors 24, 36632, 73240, 109848, 123952, 133560, 138664 and 143768; a track's length is counted in groups of eight sectors, and bit 31 of its table word marks a track that repeats (19 of the 64). In the files at 24 and 36632 every channel has 4,501 groups of audio and the sectors of submode 0 begin with group 4501. In the file at 73240 channel 7 has 1,688 groups and the channels 3 to 6 have 3,376. In the file at 138664 channel 3 has 282 groups and channel 7 has 94.
- Measured with the rig of `port/tools/test_hostcd.py` on that disc, by a private script that sends the game's sequence (Setfilter, Setmode `0xc8`, SeekL, ReadS, then `GetlocP`), with no sink:
  - the ranking's track (channel 3 from sector 138667, end 140923). Main `d0030e9d`: the position is 139424 after 600 vblanks and after 1,200, and 95 of the channel's 282 sectors were consumed. This change: 140924 after 903 vblanks, above the end, and 282 sectors.
  - a track that repeats (channel 1 from sector 73241, end 109249). Main: 86752 after 5,500 vblanks and after 14,500, and 1,689 of 4,501 sectors. This change: 109251 after 14,404 vblanks, and 4,501 sectors.
  - the first file, a track on channel 0 (end 36032) and one on channel 1 (end 36033). Main: the position stays at 36033 for both, which is above the first track's end and not above the second's, so of the tracks of that file only the one on channel 0 began again. This change: 36034 and 36035 after 14,404 vblanks.
- Change: in a read with the mode's XA bit set during which `CdReady` has not been called, a data sector does not wait: the next one replaces it in the FIFO and the drive goes on, as on the console. A call of `CdReady` during the read marks a reader that polls and one sector waits again; a new read clears the mark. With the XA bit clear nothing changes: the game's two readers that poll set the mode `0x80` first (`ps1/src/y14e890_r2.c`, `ps1/src/v6_r1.c`). Stated limit, which no code of the game meets: a reader that polls with the XA bit set loses the sectors of a vblank's row that comes before its first poll.
- What ran on this change:
  - `python3 port/tools/test_hostcd.py`: 244 cases, `all cases behaved as required` (236 before; eight new ones on a made-up audio file with a short channel and sectors of submode 0). With `--src` on main's `port/src`, six of the eight new cases fail (the other two are the cases in which one sector waits, which main does everywhere).
  - Six one-line changes of the rule in a scratch copy, each run with `--src`: the XA bit alone deciding (2 cases fail), the mark not cleared by a new read (1), the mark not set by `CdReady` (2), the XA test inverted (8), no waiting at all (6), the mark set at the read's start (6).
  - The other suites that build `cd.c`, with the i686 MinGW compiler, once each: `test_hoststream.py` (68 cases), `test_hostprogcalls.py` (49), `test_hostlaunch.py` (68) and `test_hostaudio.py` (32, with a PsyZ build of this machine: the pin with this head's `port/psyz.patch` and the private floor fix): every one `all cases behaved as required`.
  - The program: `hostbuild.py --config ps1/src/build.toml --psyz` on this change linked and verified; `sfa2.exe --list-library` ended with status 0.
- The game itself, private one-off runs with the user's disc, without a window, with `--audio-dump` and a script of button presses (no command of this repository can repeat them):
  - Training mode, Ryu against Zangief, no input after the start of the round. The stage's track is channel 0 from sector 73240. Main's program (`d0030e9d`): the dump's last frame that is not 0 is at 157.28 s, and the dump is silent from there to its end at 213 s; the track had begun near 67.2 s, so it played 90 s. This change: sound in every window of 10 s up to the dump's end at 343 s; the track fades out and begins again at 307.25 s, 240 s after its start, and the output has a second line `audio: a stream starts at sector 73240` there.
  - Arcade mode, Zangief, no input in the fight, with this change: from the lost fight through the screen "Continue?" and the track of the game's end (channel 7 from sector 138671) back to the title's module and the opening movie, with no `stop:` line in 460 s.
- Not shown in the game: the screen of the score ranking. The run above scored nothing, so the game did not show it. What issue 258 rests on is the source and the disc: the ranking's module goes on only when the music object's byte at `0x80190949` is 0 (`count_down_then_advance_step_index_slot12`, `ps1/src/slot12/slot12_3a3c_r8.c`); at a track's end it is `pause_cd_and_clear_menu_command` (`ps1/src/s14f9f0_r6.c`) that clears it, the handler of the state that the position test sets for a track that plays once; and on main the position never passed the end of the ranking's track (the measurement above).
- Not measured: how much of a track's last second is still in the queue when the game stops the track or starts it again. The queue holds up to one second and a new read empties it.
